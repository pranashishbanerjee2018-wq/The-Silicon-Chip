1. Shichman-Hodges Device Equations

Linear Region (V_DS < V_GS - V_TH):
I_D = beta * [(V_GS - V_TH) * V_DS - (V_DS^2 / 2)]

Saturation Region (V_DS ≥ V_GS - V_TH):
I_D = (beta / 2) * (V_GS - V_TH)^2

Where transconductance parameter beta = mu_n * C_ox * (W/L) = 1.000 mA/V^2, and V_TH = 0.70 V.

2. ECE Viva Examination Questions & Answers

Q1: Why does MOSFET current saturate as V_DS increases?
A: As V_DS rises, the vertical oxide field at the drain end drops to (V_GS - V_DS). When V_DS reaches V_GS - V_TH, this field drops below V_TH, causing the inversion layer to pinch off at the drain boundary. Excess voltage drops across the pinch-off space-charge region, keeping drain current clamped.

Q2: What is the physical origin of the built-in potential in a PN junction?
A: Thermal carrier diffusion across the metallurgical boundary unmasks fixed ionized dopants (NA- on P-side, ND+ on N-side). This space charge establishes an internal electric field opposing further diffusion. At thermal equilibrium, drift balances diffusion, yielding V_bi = V_T * ln(NA*ND / ni^2) ≈ 0.75 V.
